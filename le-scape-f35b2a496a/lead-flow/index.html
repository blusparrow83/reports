/ / / 01 · Overview

From Google Click To Booked Estimate

Every request from Google Search ads and from the website goes into one place, Le Scape's lead system. The person hears from Le Scape within moments, and nobody falls through the cracks after that.

  1. Someone Searches

    A Search ad for premium artificial turf shows in the Florida Panhandle (and the Nashville pilot).

  2. Landing Page

    They land on the quote page at le-scape.com/premium-turf-quote.

  3. Claim Your Free Quote

    First name, last name, email, phone, services, and two consent checkboxes.

  4. Thank You Page

    They see a confirmation, and Google Ads and Google Analytics record one lead.

  5. Lead System

    A contact and a New Lead opportunity are created and the follow up starts.

  6. Le Scape Talks To Them

    Email and text go out, your team gets a call prompt, and the lead moves through the pipeline.

Local Services Ads work alongside this. Their calls and messages arrive through Google's Local Services inbox and are tracked separately in Google Ads.

/ / / 05 · Quiet Leads

Four Emails, Seven Days Apart

Stale Leads watches the pipeline. If a lead sits with no movement, it moves to the Retarget stage and a four email series begins. Each email goes out 7 days after the last.

10Days Quiet In New Lead
7Days Quiet In Contacted
14Days Quiet In Qualified
4Emails, 7 Days Apart
The Four Emails
  • What a premium artificial turf install looks like
  • What goes into a premium install, from the turf to what is underneath
  • A few quick questions about your yard: size, pets or kids, and drainage
  • "Still thinking about your new yard?" A low pressure check in that invites them to reply "later"
Every email includes the booking link and the phone number.
/ / / 06 · AI Help

Answers On The Phone And By Text

Two AI helpers sit on top of the follow up above. They answer common questions, collect the project details, and hand every lead to Garrett with the details already in the lead system. Written and ready, waiting on one thing: the new Le Scape lead phone number. Nothing answers, texts or calls until it is attached and tested.

AI On The Phone
  • Answers calls to the Le Scape lead number as "Le Scape Assistant" and asks about the project, the space, and the best way to reach them
  • After every call a contact and an opportunity are created, tagged by the service they asked about, with a text recap to the customer and a notice to Garrett
  • Proposed call handling: during business hours the call transfers to Garrett's cell, and after hours the assistant takes a message. This needs Garrett's approval below.
AI By Text
  • Answers questions by text and helps people pick a time on the calendar
  • Starts in suggest mode. It drafts replies for your team to review and send. After we test it together, it can reply on its own.
  • Missed call text back: if a call is missed, the caller gets a friendly text right away
  • Built in limits: no prices, no guarantees, and no warranty or safety claims. Reply STOP is always honored, and anything it cannot answer goes to Garrett.
Texts also wait on business text message registration with the carriers (see the next section).
/ / / 07 · Go Live

Five Things Before Launch

Built, not yet switched on. Until these are done nothing sends or answers. All six automations are saved as drafts, and the AI helpers are written and waiting on the phone number.

  1. A Live Test

    One real submission of the quote form, followed through to the contact, the opportunity, and the first email.

  2. The Le Scape Lead Phone Number

    BluSparrow sets up a local 850 number for the lead system. Once it is attached, the AI phone assistant goes through three test calls before it answers real ones.

  3. Text Message Registration

    Business text message registration with the carriers, so the texts above can be delivered. This needs details from Garrett (below).

  4. Garrett's Approvals

    A few quick decisions from Garrett, listed below.

  5. Wording Sign Off

    Final sign off on the wording of each message, including what the AI helpers say.

Needs Garrett's Approval
  • Business details for text registration: the legal business name, the EIN (tax ID), and the business address, exactly as they appear on Le Scape's tax records. Carriers reject registrations that do not match.
  • How calls are handled: OK to have the AI answer, transfer to Garrett's cell during business hours, and take a message after hours? Please also tell us which cell number and which hours.
  • A short voicemail recording: 15 to 20 seconds in Garrett's own voice, for when a call cannot be answered.
  • The AI's limits: OK that the AI never quotes prices, never makes guarantees, and never makes warranty or safety claims, and hands those questions to Garrett.
  • Message wording: a quick read of the texts and emails on this page.
Once these are in, BluSparrow can finish setup, run the tests, and switch everything on.
