/ / / 01 · Overview

From Google Click To Booked Estimate

Every request from Google Search ads and from the website goes into one place, Le Scape's lead system. The person hears from Le Scape within moments, and nobody falls through the cracks after that.

  1. Someone Searches

    A Search ad for premium artificial turf shows from the Gulf Coast to Nashville.

  2. Landing Page

    They land on the quote page at le-scape.com/premium-turf-quote.

  3. Claim Your Free Quote

    First name, last name, email, phone, services, and two consent checkboxes.

  4. Thank You Page

    They see a confirmation, and Google Ads and Google Analytics record one lead.

  5. Lead System

    A contact and a New Lead opportunity are created and the follow up starts.

  6. Le Scape Talks To Them

    Email and text go out, your team gets a call prompt, and the lead moves through the pipeline.

Local Services Ads work alongside this. Their calls and messages arrive through Google's Local Services inbox and are tracked separately in Google Ads.

The Speed To Lead Schedule

Fast first, then spaced out: right away, within 5 minutes, at 2 hours, at 24 hours, then Day 3, 5, and 7, and the Quiet Leads series after that. Speed is what the research backs. Harvard Business Review research on web leads, as reported, found that companies that contacted a lead within an hour were nearly seven times as likely to qualify it as companies that waited longer. Each message below is matched to the lead and stops the moment they reply, book, or text STOP.

/ / / 06 · AI Help

Answers On The Phone And By Text

Two AI helpers sit on top of the follow up above. They answer common questions, collect the project details, and hand every lead to Garrett with the details already in the lead system. Written and ready, waiting on one thing: the new Le Scape lead phone number. Nothing answers, texts or calls until it is attached and tested.

AI On The Phone

A warm, polished phone assistant answers every call on the new Le Scape lead number. It works like a concierge: it greets the caller, asks a few simple questions, captures the details, and promises a call back from the team. It never quotes prices and never promises results. Calls are capped at 5 minutes, and the existing business line stays exactly as it is.

What It Says First

"Thank you for calling Le Scape, premium artificial turf from the Gulf Coast to Nashville. This is the Le Scape assistant. How can I help you today?"

How It Closes

"Thanks, [name]. I have everything. Someone from the Le Scape team will reach out soon to set up your free estimate. Is there anything else I can help with?"

What It Asks, One Question At A Time
  • Which service are you interested in?
  • What city or ZIP is the property in?
  • Is this a home or a business?
  • Roughly how big is the area, and do you have pets or family using it? (optional)
  • When are you hoping to get started?
  • Full name, best phone number, and email address, read back to confirm.

/ / / 07 · Costs

What Calls And Texts Cost

The lead system charges for phone and text usage, not for the account. Le Scape's usage is passed through at exactly what the platform charges, with no markup, and the monthly fee with BluSparrow stays the same. Each month Garrett gets the actual usage so he sees the real numbers. A payment card is added to the account and usage is charged to it. Adding Garrett as a user costs nothing extra, because users are unlimited on the plan.

/ / / 09 · Go Live

Five Things Before Launch

Built, not yet switched on. Until these are done nothing sends or answers. All six automations are saved as drafts, and the AI helpers are written and waiting on the phone number.

  1. A Live Test

    One real submission of the quote form, followed through to the contact, the opportunity, and the first email.

  2. The Le Scape Lead Phone Number

    The platform's phone service is not active on this account yet, so BluSparrow first has it activated, then sets up a local 850 number for the lead system. Once the number is attached, the AI phone assistant goes through three test calls before it answers real ones.

  3. Text Message Registration

    Business text message registration with the carriers, so the texts above can be delivered. This needs details from Garrett (below).

  4. Garrett's Approvals

    A few quick decisions from Garrett, listed below.

  5. Wording Sign Off

    Final sign off on the wording of each message, including what the AI helpers say.

Needs Garrett's Approval
  • Message wording: a yes, or any changes, on every email, text, and the phone script on this page.
  • Call transfers: confirm that (850) 490-4256 is the number where transferred calls should ring, Monday to Friday, 7 AM to 5 PM Central.
  • Usage costs: a quick written yes that calls and texts are passed through at cost, with no markup, charged to a card on the account. The agreement does not name this service, so we want it in writing.
  • New 850 number: a yes to adding a new local number just for leads (about $1.15 a month). The current business line stays as it is.
  • Text registration: the legal business name, business address, and tax ID, exactly as on Le Scape's tax records. The carriers require them before texts can be delivered.
  • Garrett's login: watch for the invite to the lead system, set a password, and download the LeadConnector app so new leads ping the phone.
  • Hear the assistant: a short test call with Garrett, and he tells us how it sounds.
  • Voicemail recording: 15 to 20 seconds in Garrett's own voice, for when a call cannot be answered.
Once these are in, BluSparrow can finish setup, run the tests, and switch everything on.
