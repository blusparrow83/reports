What happens after someone asks for a quote

Every request from Google Search ads and from the website goes into one place, Le Scape's lead system. The person hears from Le Scape within moments, and nobody falls through the cracks after that.

Built, not yet switched on The system is built and ready. It goes live after one live test and the text-message registration described at the bottom of this page.

The path from Google to a booked estimate

  1. 1Someone searchesA Search ad for premium artificial turf shows in the Florida Panhandle (and the Nashville pilot).
  2. 2Landing pageThey land on the quote page at le-scape.com/premium-turf-quote.
  3. 3Claim Your Free QuoteFirst name, last name, email, phone, services, and two consent checkboxes.
  4. 4Thank-you pageThey see a confirmation, and Google Ads and Google Analytics record one lead.
  5. 5Lead systemA contact and a New Lead opportunity are created and the follow-up starts.
  6. 6Le Scape talks to themEmail and text go out, your team gets a call prompt, and the lead moves through the pipeline.

Local Services Ads work alongside this. Their calls and messages arrive through Google's Local Services inbox and are tracked separately in Google Ads.

If they miss the appointment

An email and a text go out right away with an apology and a rebooking link. One day later a short text checks whether they would like to reschedule or have questions.

When the project is won

A review request goes out by email and by text. Three days later your team gets an internal email asking whether the customer has left a review yet, so you can follow up personally.

Leads who go quiet

Stale Leads watches the pipeline. If a lead sits with no movement, it moves to the Retarget stage and a four-email series begins. Each email goes out 7 days after the last.

When a lead counts as quiet

  • New Lead: no movement for 10 days
  • Contacted: no movement for 7 days
  • Qualified: no movement for 14 days

The four emails

  • What a premium artificial turf install looks like
  • What goes into a premium install, from the turf to what is underneath
  • A few quick questions about your yard: size, pets or kids, and drainage
  • "Still thinking about your new yard?" A low-pressure check-in that invites them to reply "later"

Every email includes the booking link and the phone number.

Before it goes live

Three things happen first.
  • A live test: one real submission of the quote form, followed through to the contact, the opportunity, and the first email.
  • Business text-message registration with the carriers, so the texts above can be delivered.
  • Final sign-off on the wording of each message.

Until then nothing sends. All six automations are saved as drafts.
