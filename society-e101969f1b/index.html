What Happened Along The Way

  • September 14: ad funnel launched with the guide download and the traffic ad.
  • October 19 to 25: $117 spent, 204 clicks, 9 real leads.
  • October 22 to November 2: the guide ad was off, so Meta delivered nothing from October 23 to November 1 and website visits fell (44 vs 137 in the weekly email). Source: Ads Manager history and the Meta pull.
  • November 2: guide ad back on, plus a new retargeting ad for people who visited the site (early read on November 4: $25 spent, 15 clicks, 3 leads).
  • Now: ads run $38 a day across two ad sets, about $1,140 a month, above the $500 a month test.

Funnel Update: Adding A Discovery Call

Sending a church an ad and having them click straight to a $2,000 purchase does not always work. We are adding a discovery call step so we can talk first and match the right build to each church.

In the newest 40 CRM records (read November 4), no one has booked a build from these leads yet. The call is how we turn guide downloads into projects.
