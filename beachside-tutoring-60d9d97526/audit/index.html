/ / / 01 · The Verdict

The Reputation Is Built,
The Website Is Next

Beachside already has the hard part: 5.0 stars across 47 Google reviews, a real office in Harvest Village, a warm, well-designed website, and program pages that are thoughtfully written. What is missing is search work that lets Google, and the families searching on it, actually see all of that.

We reviewed the site, search visibility, local profile, social channels and competitors. The design is a keeper. The biggest finding is in how the pages are assembled: the text sits inside embedded frames, and the pages are heavy enough that a phone on a slower connection waits more than 40 seconds to see anything. The plan below keeps your design and TutorBird, starts with search fixes, and only recommends a rebuild if testing shows it is needed.

/ / / 07 · The Roadmap

Quick Wins, Then
Build, Then Scale

Three phases, cheapest and fastest first. Phase 1 can start right away alongside the social content plan. Phases 2 and 3 are where the search work compounds.

Phase 1

Quick Wins

First 30 days, inside your current tools
  • Replace the placeholder address and 555 phone in the code with your real details
  • Fix the Instagram link in the code
  • Swap the header logo for the dark version so it shows on cream
  • Review the robots.txt blocks on ACT, SAT, CLT and the privacy policy
  • Remove the duplicate description tags and the second GA4 tag
  • Add alt text to homepage images and titles to frames
  • Connect Search Console and confirm GA4 conversions
  • Plan the November address change across Google, Facebook, Instagram and the site
Phase 2

Build

Months 2 to 4, search foundation
  • Day 45 checkpoint: run the four tests, then decide to stay on the current build or rebuild the front end
  • One page per program and per community: Navarre, Gulf Breeze and Fort Walton Beach first, Milton as a later add
  • Unique titles and descriptions on every page, complete sitemap
  • Real review text and your 5.0 rating shown on the page
  • Lighter images and caching to bring page weight down
  • TutorBird and your design kept throughout
  • Timed with the new office
Phase 3

Scale

Months 4 to 12, compounding
  • Monthly SEO: new pages and posts from your free resources
  • Local links from schools, clubs and business groups
  • Google review engine after each strong result
  • Meta ads timed to test dates and enrollment windows
  • Google Ads for high-intent searches near Navarre
  • Quarterly check against the benchmarks in this audit
How this fits the proposalThe year-one plan in our proposal covers the social content. Search work is the next step on the growth ladder in that proposal (the Full-Funnel Scale tier), with paid advertising following when the site converts well. We would scope it after the Phase 1 tests and pace it to your budget and to the move. Rankings and traffic are not guaranteed, and results build over months, not days.
