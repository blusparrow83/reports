1. Your Goal, In Your Words

You want criminal defense to be the center of the firm again, so that when someone has a criminal problem, Hutchinson & Huffman is the call they make. You want the phones ringing again.

This plan is built around that. Criminal defense gets the ad dollars. Civil and personal injury get none. Your website, Google Business Profile, articles and social posts keep building the same message in the background, so the ads and the reputation work together.

2. What We Will Do

  • Run three small campaigns that put your firm in front of people searching for a DUI lawyer, a criminal defense attorney, and Marchman Act help in Palm Beach County.
  • Send each ad to its own page on your site, written to turn a visitor into a phone call.
  • Track every call so we know which searches produce real inquiries.
  • You approve every ad before it runs. Wording follows Florida Bar advertising rules. Your experience is described only the way you approve it.

3. A Straight Answer On Case Size

You asked whether ads can bring higher-value criminal cases, not only DUI volume. Honest answer: DUI searches are where the search volume is, so they are the right place to start. Serious felony clients usually find a lawyer through reputation, referrals and your articles, which we are building organically. The ads include a small criminal defense campaign to test that search too. At day 30 we show you the searches people actually typed, and we shift dollars toward whatever brings the calls you want.
